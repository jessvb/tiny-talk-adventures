I want to create a voice-based story writing app for babies and kids. It's a fun way to learn (ed-tech) about
  animals, biology, ecosystems, etc. and allows the child to be a part of the story writing alongside an LLM. Here's
  the idea so far, but I would welcome other ideas too! First, the child chooses an animal and the system searches a
  database, web api, etc. for facts about the animal. These will be incorporated into the story in a natural way, and
  serve as inspiration for the story, which is generated step-by-step by an LLM and the child together. Other
  inspiration for the story should come from the child's own environment. E.g. if there's a bookcase in the child's
  environment, perhaps the LLM says something like, "There was a tall building, that looks kind of like that
  bookcase!" or "A mountain, just like your couch". (The app has a local, on-device object recognition model for
  this.) The story should include exposition, rising action, climax, falling action and resolution, but should also
  take input from the child. For example, the story agent should ask the child what they think the animal should do
  next, similar to a choose-your-own-adventure. The child should also be able to interrupt the agent at any time
  (e.g. with a dialog agent, like Moshi) and change the story or ask about it. As much as possible, data should stay
  on the device for safety reasons, since the user is a child. There should also be safety boundaries and scaffolding
  in place for the LLM. All AI models should be free to use. The story should include visuals, ideally from real
  artists (not generated) with attribution. Perhaps at the end, an in-app story book is created that the child can go
  back to and flip through. Let me know what you think and how we can make this idea better!
